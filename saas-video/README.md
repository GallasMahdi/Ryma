# Digital Clínica — LinkedIn product overview

`digital-clinica-square.mp4`: 40 seconds, 1080 × 1080, 24 fps, H.264 video with AAC stereo audio. All editorial text is burned in. No voiceover.

## Timeline

- 0–4s: “Still running your clinic across spreadsheets?” Main dashboard.
- 4–8s: Digital Clínica positioning: scheduling, patient records and billing in one platform.
- 8–28s: Six feature views: appointments, availability, patient records, recurring sessions, billing, analytics.
- 28–34s: “From appointments to revenue.” Connected operational view.
- 34–40s: Original screenshot logo, brand name, tagline and “See it in action.”

The video uses supplied screenshots, slow camera pushes, understated gold highlight boxes and simple cuts. Contact details and visible patient names in used regions are concealed or excluded by framing. Dashboard figures are source UI values, not claims of customer results. No website or performance claim was invented.

`ambient-original.wav` is an original synthesized pad soundtrack generated by the included script; no third-party music recording was used. `captions.srt` contains the editorial headline and supporting text. `storyboard.json` records timing and crops. `contact-sheet.jpg` shows the ten scenes. `poster.jpg` is the opening poster.

`render.py` recreates the export with Python, Pillow, NumPy, Windows Segoe UI fonts and the local FFmpeg executable. Source screenshots are read from the original upload paths in the script.

## 💡 5. How to Conduct the 15-Minute Live Demo Meeting

1. **Minutes 0–3 (Problem Context):**  
   Highlight the local Lisbon market insight: *"In Lisbon, 40% of patients try to book appointments in the evening. If reception is closed or busy, they return to Google and book with a competitor."*

2. **Minutes 3–8 (Platform Demo):**  
   Open `http://localhost:3001/index.html` on your laptop/tablet. Show the ultra-fast load time (<1 sec), the 3D visual aesthetics, and demonstrate a patient booking an appointment in **90 seconds**. Highlight the **GDPR/CNPD medical data compliance badge**.

3. **Minutes 8–12 (Interactive ROI Calculation):**  
   Open `http://localhost:3001/roi-report.html#calculator`. Adjust the live range sliders with the clinic's actual numbers (e.g. 80 consultations/month @ €70/consultation). Show them on-screen that they **recoup their €4,500 investment in 2.3 months**.

4. **Minutes 12–15 (Closing the Deal):**  
   Offer the 5-week launch guarantee: *"We guarantee a complete launch in 5 weeks on your .pt domain with full staff training included. Shall we get started this week?"*
